Sadhna - To accomplish - Goal oriented methodical discipline with spiritual insights

Team Name: Legacy Loop

project Description:
We want to build Sadhana OS, a personal and family habit operating system. It will help individuals track and improve habits across spiritual practice, yoga, exercise, professional growth, family, personal discipline, social commitments, and wellbeing.

Unlike simple streak-based habit trackers, it will support customizable categories and sub-categories, habit history, audit trails, reflections, progress reviews, and personalized routines for each family member.

This matters because people do not just need another to-do list. They need a system that connects daily actions with values, discipline, family goals, and long-term growth. Eventually, we want to integrate frameworks like Atomic Habits, 7 Habits, and AI-assisted coaching to create a complete operating system for intentional and productive living.
