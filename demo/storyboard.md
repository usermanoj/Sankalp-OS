# Sankalp OS Demo Storyboard

Target length: 2 minutes 12 seconds

1. **Sankalp OS in one view** (13s)
   Daily action, family routines, review intelligence, and journaling in a calm premium workspace.

2. **SMART practice, not simple streaks** (14s)
   Each habit carries target, frequency, type-aware tracking, SMART criteria, notes, and seven-day rhythm.

3. **Focused domain mode** (13s)
   Selecting a domain removes global review noise and keeps the page action-oriented for that life area.

4. **Habit Studio** (14s)
   Build and amend the life operating model: domains, sub-components, family assignments, and routines.

5. **Create any habit** (13s)
   Add a new habit, choose tracking type, define measurable criteria, and create new sub-components on the fly.

6. **Whole-day journaling** (14s)
   Capture the story of the day with free-flow reflection, wins, blockers, and tomorrow's adjustment.

7. **Review Center insights** (17s)
   Review trends, domain balance, habit consistency, and signals over 30 days, 90 days, 1 year, or 5 years.

8. **Professional history and export** (18s)
   Day-by-day history preserves habit entries, notes, daily journals, archived records, and exportable reports.

9. **Audit-proof life system** (16s)
   Archives, restores, tracking, edits, and journal saves are kept in a transparent audit trail.
